THE RISE OF A LEGEND

Gaddafi Stadium ke ball boy se dunya ke top-ranked batsman banne tak ka tareechi safar.

07
2007 - Ball Boy Era

Gaddafi Stadium Ball Boy

Babar Azam started his passion at Gaddafi Stadium Lahore as a ball boy during South Africa's 2007 tour of Pakistan. Fetching balls outside the boundary sparked his lifelong dream to bat for his country.

12
2012 - Under-19 Captain

Leading National Youth Team

Captained Pakistan in the 2012 ICC Under-19 Cricket World Cup in Australia. Finished as Pakistan's top run-scorer with 287 runs.

15
May 31, 2015 - International Debut

ODI Debut vs Zimbabwe

Made his ODI debut in Lahore against Zimbabwe, scoring a fluent 54 runs off 60 balls and instantly proving his international credentials.

16
2016 - 3 Consecutive ODI Centuries

World Record vs West Indies

Scored 3 back-to-back ODI centuries (120, 123, 117) against West Indies in UAE, becoming only the 3rd Pakistani batter to achieve this feat.

19
2019 - Captaincy & World Supremacy

All-Format Captain & ICC Player of the Year

Appointed captain in all formats, leading Pakistan to the T20 World Cup 2022 Final. Awarded Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy & Sitara-e-Imtiaz for unmatched international performance.
